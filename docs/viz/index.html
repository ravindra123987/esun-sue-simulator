Scale-up Packet Viewer: XPU NIC → switch port

One hop of a scale-up network, replayed from the discrete-event simulator. XPU A's on-die SUE NIC sends 1 KB frames over an 800G cable to port 1 of an ESUN switch, which buffers them and forwards them out of port 7. Every packet, control block, counter and buffer slot below comes from the trace; frames on the cable are drawn to scale.

How the model maps to the hardware. The simulator models one full-duplex link. Its sender is drawn as XPU A's NIC: an always-full send queue in HBM, the CBFC credit gate, LLR transmit with its replay buffer, and the MAC/PCS/SerDes. Its receiver is drawn as switch port 1: FCS check and LLR receive, then the lossless ingress buffer that CBFC credits describe. The receiver's drain rate is drawn as the egress port forwarding at that rate. Control blocks (8 B) ride in fixed slots every 2048 B of link time and are drawn wider than their true 0.08 ns.
Not modelled yet (drawn dimmed): the switch's parse/lookup/scheduling latency (0 ns here), the second hop to XPU B, and DMA timing from HBM. One VC, both ends start initialised, no PCS-status timer. Traces: tools/make_traces.py; captions: sim/trace.py; limits: docs/experiments/llr-cbfc.md.
